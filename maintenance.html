Open your boat maintenance data

This app keeps your records in ordinary JSON/JSONL files inside one portable ZIP. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Your maintenance data is a normal ZIP containing readable JSON/JSONL files and attachments. Where supported, saving writes back to the ZIP you opened. On browsers that cannot overwrite it, the app protects the newer browser working copy and warns before opening an older ZIP.
